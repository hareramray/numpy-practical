The same 4 by 3 scores table four times; blue cells are the ones each expression selects. A single pair of integers picks one cell. A slice in the row position picks a band of rows and keeps every column. A colon in the row position with an integer column picks a vertical strip. A step of 2 skips every other row: the notebook prints rows [65 80 90] and [95 88 92]. Ask the audience to point at the cells before revealing each grid. Try it: shade scores[-2:, :2] yourself: the bottom two rows, left two columns, giving [[95 88] [50 70]].
