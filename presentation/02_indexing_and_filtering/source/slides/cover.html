Welcome to notebook 02. In notebook 01 we learned to read an array's shape. Now we use that shape to pick data out: single values, ranges of rows and columns, values that meet a condition, and specific combinations of rows and columns. The motif on this slide shows the two big ideas: a blue column selected by a slice, and scattered orange cells selected by a condition.
