The idea to remember

Slices keep shape.
Masks pick values.

Select by position with integers and slices, by condition with Boolean masks joined by &, | and ~.

Pause here. Every selection in NumPy is one of these two kinds. Position-based selection, with integers and slices, follows the shape: slices keep the axes, integers remove them. Condition-based selection, with a Boolean mask, asks a question of every element and keeps the ones that answer True, returning a flat 1D result. Knowing which kind you are writing tells you the shape of the answer before you run it.
