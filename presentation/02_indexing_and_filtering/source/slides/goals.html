The notebook's goal is to select individual values, slice tables, and combine Boolean conditions. Each card is one step. Position-based selection uses integers and slices. Condition-based selection uses a Boolean array of the same shape. Index lists let you pick arbitrary rows and columns, and they come with a trap we will draw out on a grid: two lists pair up coordinates rather than forming a rectangle.
