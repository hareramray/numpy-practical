Draw this cube before writing any analysis code. Axis 0 is the day, axis 1 the store and axis 2 the product, so units[2, 0, 1] reads as day index 2 (the third day), store 0 (North), product 1 (Coffee). The two orange cells are the only missing counts in 360 entries. Every later question is a choice of axes: per store means collapse days and products, per product collapses days and stores, per day collapses stores and products. Naming the axes now makes those reductions obvious later.
