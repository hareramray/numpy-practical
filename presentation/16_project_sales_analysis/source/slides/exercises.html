Ask the audience to attempt both before revealing the code lines, which match the reference solutions. Exercise 1: dividing each store total by the grand total gives fractions that sum to one; North earns about 34.6 percent. Exercise 2: a mean over the day axis leaves the store and product axes, shape (3, 4). The check multiplies by 30 to recover revenue.sum(axis=0), which is a neat way to confirm you averaged over the right axis. North's Coffee figure, 362.7586, has odd decimals because one of its days is an imputed estimate, an average over 29 observed days.
