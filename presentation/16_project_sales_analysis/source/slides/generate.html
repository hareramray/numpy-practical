A seeded generator

Seed 2026 gives everyone the same month of sales.

One rate per product

lam broadcasts along the last axis: about 12, 8, 6 and 15 units a day.

NaN is not zero

It marks a count we never observed. astype(float) makes room for it.

The brief: three stores, four products, 30 days. Poisson counts model whole units sold per day, and the rate vector of length 4 broadcasts against the size (30, 3, 4), so each product gets its own average. Poisson returns integers, which cannot hold NaN, so the cube is converted to float before two counts are blanked out. Ask the audience to predict the shape and the missing count before running. Try it: add a fifth product. The rate vector, the price vector and the product names must all grow to length 5, and the shape becomes (30, 3, 5); forget one and broadcasting or the report breaks.
