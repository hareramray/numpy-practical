Read each row as: which axes disappear, and which one survives. The first row is broadcasting: a length 4 price vector stretches across 30 days and 3 stores. The store row keeps axis 1 and shows the real totals; North leads at 31487.76. The product row keeps axis 2, and argmax picks Coffee, the most expensive product at 40 per unit even though Tea and Water sell more units. The daily row keeps axis 0; its argmax is index 8, which is day 9 because days are numbered from 1 in the report. All three rows add up to the same grand total, 91119.66, which is the reconciliation check.
