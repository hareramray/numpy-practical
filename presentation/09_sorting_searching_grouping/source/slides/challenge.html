The challenge chains all three steps. Start with two arrays of equal length: a product label for each sale and its value. Use np.unique with return_inverse=True to get product codes, then np.bincount with weights for totals and without weights for counts; totals divided by counts gives the mean sale value. Rank the products with a stable argsort of the negated totals and apply that one order to products, totals, counts and means so every row stays aligned. There is no supplied check: assert that totals.sum() matches the raw sum with np.testing.assert_allclose, and check that the first-ranked product has the largest total. Then restart the kernel and run all cells once more.
