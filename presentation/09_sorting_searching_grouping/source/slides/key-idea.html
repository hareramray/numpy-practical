The idea to remember

Work with positions,
not just values.

argsort, searchsorted and unique return integers. Reuse them to keep arrays aligned and to reduce by group.

Pause here. Every tool in this notebook answers with integers rather than values: argsort gives an order, searchsorted gives a gap number, unique gives group codes. Those integers are reusable. One order reorders every related array, two insertion points count duplicates, and one code array lets bincount total, count and average any column by group. Thinking in positions is what makes NumPy feel like a database without one.
