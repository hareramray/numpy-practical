This is the picture to keep in your head for every reduction in the course. On the left, axis=0 runs down the rows, so each column's two values are added: 1+4, 2+5, 3+6 give the orange [5 7 9]. On the right, axis=1 runs across the columns, so each row's three values are added: 1+2+3 and 4+5+6 give the blue [6 15]. In both cases the axis you name disappears from the shape: (2, 3) loses axis 0 to become (3,), or loses axis 1 to become (2,). Try it, as the notebook suggests: draw a 2 by 3 grid on paper and label which entries contribute to each of the five totals.
