NumPy course · 03 Shapes and axes

5

Transpose rearranges axes. For a 2D table, .T exchanges them: the element at row i, column j moves to row j, column i, so a (2, 3) array becomes (3, 2). Follow the colours: the blue first row 1, 2, 3 becomes the first column, and the orange second row becomes the second column. Unlike reshape, transpose changes the reading order: reading points.T row by row gives 1, 4, 2, 5, 3, 6. That is why reshape(3, 2) and .T give different results even though both have shape (3, 2). Ask the audience to predict points.reshape(3, 2): [[1 2] [3 4] [5 6]].
