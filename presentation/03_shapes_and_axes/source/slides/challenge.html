The challenge has no supplied check: write your own. With shape (2, 7, 3), a total per store keeps axis 0 and collapses the other two, so pass axis=(1, 2) and expect shape (2,). A total per day keeps axis 1: axis=(0, 2), shape (7,). A total per product keeps axis 2: axis=(0, 1), shape (3,). A good self-check: all three results must add up to the same grand total, sales.sum(). Then restart the kernel and run all cells once more.
