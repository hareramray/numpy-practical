The idea to remember

Name the axis
that disappears.

reshape keeps every value, .T swaps the axes, and a reduction collapses exactly the axis you name.

Pause here. From notebook 04 onwards almost every function takes an axis argument: sum, mean, max, sort, concatenate and more. The rule is always the same: the axis you pass is the one the operation runs along, and for reductions it is the axis that disappears from the shape. When unsure, write the shape tuple down and cross out the axis you are naming; what remains is the shape of the answer.
