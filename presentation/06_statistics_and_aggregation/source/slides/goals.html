The notebook's goal: choose reduction axes, summarise distributions, and build cumulative metrics. A reduction turns many values into fewer, such as a whole array into one mean, or a table into one total per row. The three steps go from a single list of values, to a two-dimensional table where you must pick the axis, to summaries that run along the data, such as running totals.
