This is the same five deliveries on a number line of minutes. The four black marks crowd together between 20 and 23; the fifth sits alone at 90. The blue median line sits inside the crowd at 22, and the blue box, the middle half of the data between the first and third quartiles, is only two minutes wide. The dashed orange mean line has been pulled right to 35.2, where no delivery actually is. The orange band spans one population standard deviation, 27.42 minutes, either side of the mean, from about 7.8 to 62.6: the outlier inflates it too. When data has outliers, report the median and quartiles alongside, or instead of, the mean and standard deviation.
