Here is the sales table with both reductions drawn around it. Summing along axis=1 walks across each row, so each day collapses to one blue total on the right: 5 + 3 + 2 = 10 for day one. Summing along axis=0 walks down each column, so each product collapses to one orange total underneath: 5 + 7 + 4 + 8 = 24 for tea. Whichever axis you name is the one that vanishes from the shape. The outlined 24 is the maximum; argmax reports its position, 0, and products[0] is tea. Both sets of totals add to the same grand total of 53, which is what the notebook's assert verifies.
