Prerequisites: notebooks 00–05

Notebook 06 turns arrays into answers. You will describe the centre and spread of a set of values, choose which axis of a table to reduce, and build weighted averages, running totals and day-to-day changes. The cover's growing bars preview the last idea, a cumulative sum. As always, the notebook imports NumPy and creates its own data.
