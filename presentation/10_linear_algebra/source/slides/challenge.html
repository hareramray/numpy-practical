The challenge has no supplied check: write your own. Build a design matrix with three columns, ones, x and x squared, using np.column_stack, generate noisy data with a seeded random generator, and fit with lstsq. Then fit the two-column straight-line model to the same data and compare the residuals. The quadratic will never have a higher training error, because it can always set c to zero and reproduce the line. That is exactly why lower training error alone is insufficient: extra flexibility can fit the noise rather than the pattern. A fair comparison uses held-out data the model never saw.
