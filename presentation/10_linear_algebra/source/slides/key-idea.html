The idea to remember

Match the shapes.
Solve, don't invert.

Check (m, n) @ (n, p) before you multiply. For A x = b, call solve or lstsq, then check the residual.

Pause here. Almost every linear algebra bug in NumPy is either a shape mismatch or trusting a computed answer without checking it. Read the shapes before writing @. Prefer np.linalg.solve for square systems and np.linalg.lstsq when there are more equations than unknowns, and always verify with a residual or an assert_allclose.
