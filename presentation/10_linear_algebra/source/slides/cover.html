Welcome to notebook 10. A matrix is a rectangular table of numbers and a vector is a 1D array. In this notebook we multiply matrices, solve linear systems, fit a straight line with least squares, and look inside a matrix with the singular value decomposition. The motif on this slide is the core move of the whole topic: one row crossing one column to produce a single entry of a matrix product.
