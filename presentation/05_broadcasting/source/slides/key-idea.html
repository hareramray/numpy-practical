The idea to remember

Align right.
Ones stretch.

Compare shapes from the last axis: each pair equal, or one of them 1. Write the shape beside every step.

Pause here. Every broadcasting question is answered by the same check: line the shapes up on the right, and each pair of dimensions must be equal or contain a 1. A length-1 axis, whether it comes from keepdims, from indexing with None, or from a missing leading dimension, is the axis that stretches. When a calculation surprises you, write the shape of every intermediate array; the mistake is almost always visible there.
