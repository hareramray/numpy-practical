NumPy course · 05 Broadcasting

4

Three customers are rows and two products are columns. The tax rates have shape (2,), one per product. Aligned from the right, 2 matches 2 and the missing row axis acts as 1, so the rates are reused for every customer: 100 becomes 105 at 5 percent and 200 becomes 220 at 10 percent. You never build a 3 by 2 array of rates; NumPy behaves as if the row were repeated. The full (3, 2) result is still allocated, so broadcasting saves input copies, not output memory.
