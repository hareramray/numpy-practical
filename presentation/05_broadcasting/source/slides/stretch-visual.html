Both results come from the notebook's Step 1 cell. On the left, the two tax factors are stored once as a row; the dashed rows show how broadcasting reuses that row for each of the three customers, so 150 times 1.05 gives 157.5 in the middle row. On the right, the notebook writes discount_by_customer[:, None] to turn three discounts into a (3, 1) column; the dashed column shows that column reused for both products, so customer two pays 150 minus 20, which is 130, and 250 minus 20, which is 230. A row stretches down; a column stretches across. The direction is decided entirely by where the length-1 axis sits.
