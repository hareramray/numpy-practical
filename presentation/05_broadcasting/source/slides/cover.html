Broadcasting

Calculate across dimensions

Intermediate

30–50 minutes

Prerequisites: notebooks 00–04

Notebook 05 extends elementwise arithmetic to arrays of different shapes. You will learn to predict whether two shapes are compatible, to centre rows and columns of a table with keepdims, and to build every pairwise difference between two sets of values. The cover grid previews the idea: one orange column and one blue row are enough to describe a whole grid of results.
