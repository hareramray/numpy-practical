NumPy course · 05 Broadcasting

2

The notebook's goal: predict compatible shapes, and use broadcasting for normalisation and distance. Broadcasting is how NumPy combines arrays whose shapes differ, such as a table and one value per column, without you repeating data by hand. Everything rests on one rule about shapes, which comes first. Then two everyday uses: centring a table by subtracting means, and computing all pairwise differences between two sets of values.
