This is the bootstrap as a picture. The blue row is the observed sample, mean 13.5. Each grey and orange row is one row of resampled: eight draws from the observed values, with replacement. These are the first three rows produced by the notebook's own code with seed 10. Replacement is what makes rows differ: in row 0 the value 18 is drawn three times while 12, 15 and 11 never appear, so its mean rises to 15.0; row 2 draws 12 twice and lands at 13.625. Each row's mean is one plausible value of the sample mean, and 2,000 of them describe how much the mean could move. The 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles of those means give the interval 11.75 to 15.375. Try it: plot the 2,000 means with plt.hist to see their spread around 13.5.
