Randomness and simulation

Seeded generators, Monte Carlo and the bootstrap

Intermediate

30–50 minutes

Prerequisites: notebooks 00–06

This notebook is about reproducible randomness. You will create a local random Generator, draw samples from common distributions, estimate a probability by simulation, and bootstrap a sample mean. The thread through all of it: a seed makes a random run repeatable, and many random trials, averaged, let you estimate quantities that a single draw cannot tell you. As always, the notebook imports its own libraries and creates its own data.
