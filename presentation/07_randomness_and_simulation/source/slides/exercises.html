Two simulation exercises

Exercise 1

100 die rolls from a generator seeded with 99

Expected: shape (100,), integers 1 to 6, starting 6 4 5 4 2 4 …

rolls = np.random.default_rng(99)\
    .integers(1, 7, size=100)

Exercise 2

Share of coin trials with at least one head

Expected: 0.75. Trials [[0,0],[0,1],[1,0],[1,1]], where 1 means heads.

probability = (coin_trials == 1)\
    .any(axis=1).mean()

Let the audience try both before revealing the code, which matches the reference solutions split over two lines. Exercise 1: integers excludes its high bound, so pass 7 to allow a 6; a fresh generator with seed 99 reproduces exactly the sequence the check expects, beginning 6, 4, 5, 4, 2, 4. Exercise 2: any(axis=1) marks each trial that contains a head, giving False, True, True, True; the mean of those Booleans is the fraction of successful trials, 0.75. That is the Monte Carlo pattern from step 2 on a tiny, hand-checkable array.
