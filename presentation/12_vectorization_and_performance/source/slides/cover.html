Welcome to notebook 12. Vectorization means moving repeated numeric work out of Python loops and into array operations that run in compiled code. It is often dramatically faster, but this notebook teaches the discipline around it: prove the fast version is correct, measure speed fairly instead of assuming it, and keep an eye on the temporary arrays that vectorized expressions create. The cover contrasts a whole row processed at once, in orange, with single cells visited one at a time, in blue.
