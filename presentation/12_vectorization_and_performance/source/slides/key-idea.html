The idea to remember

Check first.
Then measure.

Vectorize against a slow reference, time repeated runs, and watch the temporary arrays your expressions create.

Pause here. A fast wrong answer is worthless, and a speed claim without a measurement is a guess. Keep a simple reference implementation, prove agreement with assert_allclose, benchmark with repeated runs and report the setup, and remember that every intermediate array costs memory. Reuse buffers with out= or process chunks when the data gets large.
