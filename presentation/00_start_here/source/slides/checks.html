Every exercise in the course follows this pattern. You replace None with your answer and rerun the check cell. The check verifies your result, not your method, so any correct approach passes. If an assertion fails, read the expected value and inspect your shape and dtype. For arrays, the checks use np.testing.assert_allclose, which allows tiny floating-point differences.
