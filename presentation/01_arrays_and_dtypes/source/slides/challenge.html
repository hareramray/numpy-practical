The challenge has no supplied check: write your own. A natural layout puts students on axis 0 and subjects on axis 1, giving shape (4, 3), ndim 2 and size 12. With the default int64 dtype that is 12 times 8, 96 bytes; stored as float64 it is also 96, and as int8 it would be 12 bytes, if every score fits in -128 to 127.
