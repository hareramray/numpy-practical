Arrays and

Create arrays and understand data types

Beginner

30–50 minutes

Prerequisites: Notebook 00

Welcome to notebook 01. Notebook 00 showed that arrays apply one operation to every element. Now we look inside an array: its shape, its number of dimensions, its size, and its dtype, the single data type every element shares. We also meet the constructors you will use in every later notebook. As always, the notebook imports NumPy itself and creates its own data, so it runs on its own.
