2

The notebook's goal: use tolerances, avoid overflow and cancellation, and inspect precision limits. Each of these is a place where the computer's arithmetic differs from the arithmetic on paper. None of them is a NumPy bug; they follow from storing numbers in a fixed number of bits. Knowing them lets you write tests that pass for the right reasons and formulas that stay accurate for extreme inputs.
