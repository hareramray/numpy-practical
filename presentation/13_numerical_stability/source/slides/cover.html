Welcome to notebook 13. Computers store real numbers as binary floating-point values with a fixed number of bits, so most decimals are approximations and every dtype has limits. This notebook shows how to compare such numbers sensibly, how to keep tiny changes from vanishing, how integers overflow, and how to compute softmax without overflowing. Like every notebook, it imports its own libraries and creates its own data.
