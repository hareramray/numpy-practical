NumPy integers have a fixed width. An int8 has 8 bits, so it holds 256 values, from -128 to 127. The bar shows that range: starting at 120 (blue), adding 120 runs past 127, wraps around to -128 and keeps counting until it lands on -16 (orange), which is 240 minus 256. NumPy array arithmetic does not raise an error for this; it silently wraps. The fix is to promote the dtype before the arithmetic that might overflow: with int64 the sum is 240. Watch for this with small dtypes such as int8, uint8 image pixels or int16 sensor data, especially when summing or multiplying.
