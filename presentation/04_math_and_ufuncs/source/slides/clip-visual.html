Read the diagram top to bottom, one column at a time. The two dashed scores lie outside the interval, so clip moves them to the nearest bound, shown in orange; the middle values are untouched. Comparing bounded with 60 gives a boolean mask of the same shape. np.where then walks the mask: True positions take "pass", False positions take "retry". Every step keeps the shape of four cells, which is why the operations chain together without loops. Point out that clipping first matters: without it, the score of 110 would still pass, but -5 would be a meaningless score.
