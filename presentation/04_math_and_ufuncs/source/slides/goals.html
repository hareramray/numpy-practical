The notebook's goal in one line: calculate elementwise, use ufuncs, and handle division deliberately. The three steps build on each other. First, operators and universal functions apply to every element. Second, clip and where let you decide what each element becomes. Third, the where= argument of a ufunc lets you skip calculations that would be invalid, such as dividing by zero.
