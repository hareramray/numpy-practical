Learn NumPy by doing · Notebook 04

Math and ufuncs

Elementwise arithmetic, conditions and safe division

Beginner

30–50 minutes

Prerequisites: notebooks 00–03

Notebook 04 is about array arithmetic. You will calculate element by element with ordinary operators and universal functions, choose an output per element with np.where and np.clip, and divide safely when some denominators are zero. The decorative grid hints at the idea: two rows of inputs combine, cell by cell, into a row of results. As always, the notebook imports NumPy and creates its own data.
