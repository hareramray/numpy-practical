The challenge combines all three steps. Growth from day i to day i+1 is (sales[1:] - sales[:-1]) / sales[:-1] times 100, an elementwise calculation on two shifted slices. A zero on the earlier day makes the division undefined, so use np.divide with out= set to a NaN-filled buffer and where= set to the previous day being nonzero, exactly as in Step 3. Pick a short series that includes a zero, work out two or three growth values by hand, and assert them. Then restart the kernel and run all cells once more.
