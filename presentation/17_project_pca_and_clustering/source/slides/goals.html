NumPy course · 17 PCA and clustering project

2

The notebook's goal: standardise features, implement PCA with SVD, and build a checked k-means loop. PCA finds directions of variation; clustering groups nearby observations. Both draw on almost everything in the course: axis reductions from 03 and 06, broadcasting from 05, SVD from 10, and the numerical care of 13. The fourth card matters as much as the algorithms: a home-made implementation is only trustworthy when its invariants are tested, such as reconstruction error, orthonormal directions, and an objective that never increases.
