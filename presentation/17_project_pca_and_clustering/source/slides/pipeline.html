The workflow

From 3 features to 3 clusters

Step 1

Scale

Standardise: each feature to mean 0, std 1

Step 2

SVD

Covariance directions as rows of Vt

Step 2

Project

Keep two directions: shape (180, 2)

Step 3

K-means

Assign, update, repeat until stable

Step 3

Evaluate

Best of 5 seeds, sizes, tests, plot

Each stage asserts an invariant: mean 0 and std 1, exact SVD reconstruction, orthonormal directions, a non-increasing objective.

This is the map for the session. The data are 180 observations with three numeric features, drawn around three hidden centres. Stage one standardises the features. Stage two takes the SVD of the standardised table; its right singular vectors are the eigenvectors of the covariance matrix, so this is the same PCA you may have seen defined through covariance, computed more stably. Stage three keeps the first two directions, giving a 2-D view. Stage four runs k-means, and stage five chooses the best run, reports cluster sizes, runs edge-case tests and plots the clusters in the PCA view. In the notebook, k-means clusters the standardised 3-D table Z; the 2-D projection is used for the picture.
