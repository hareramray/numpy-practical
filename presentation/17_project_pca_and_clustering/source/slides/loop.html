This is the whole algorithm. Inertia is the total squared distance from each point to its assigned center. Assigning each point to its nearest center cannot increase it, because each point picks the smallest available distance. Moving each center to its group's mean cannot increase it either, because the mean is the point that minimises squared distance to a group. So inertia never rises, and since there are finitely many labelings the loop must stop. The notebook turns this argument into a test: np.diff(history) must be at most 1e-9 everywhere. What the loop does not promise is the best answer overall: it finds a local minimum that depends on the starting rows, which is why we try several seeds.
