Prerequisites: notebooks 00–14

Welcome to notebook 15. A sampled signal is just a 1D array of values recorded at evenly spaced times. The fast Fourier transform, np.fft, rewrites that array as a sum of sine waves of different frequencies, which makes it easy to see which frequencies are present and to remove the ones you do not want. The only extra prerequisite is knowing that a sine wave repeats at a frequency measured in cycles per second, or hertz. The notebook imports its own libraries, including matplotlib for the three plots you will see.
