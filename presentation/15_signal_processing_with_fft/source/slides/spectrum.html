These are the notebook's two plots, one above the other. The top one is the signal in time: 256 samples whose two ingredients are mixed together. The bottom one is the same information after rfft, shown as a stem plot from 0 to 60 Hz, where each dot is one 1 Hz bin. Two spikes stand out: height 1.0 at 5 Hz and height 0.4 at 40 Hz, matching the printed peak frequencies and amplitudes. Nothing was lost in the transform; it is a change of viewpoint, and the inverse transform can bring the time view back exactly. Frequency is the natural view when you ask what is in a signal, time is the natural view when you ask what happened when.
