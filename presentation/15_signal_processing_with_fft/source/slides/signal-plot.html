This is the plot the notebook draws in Step 1. Count the large swings: five per second, the 5 Hz component with amplitude 1. On top of each swing sits a fast, smaller wiggle: the 40 Hz component with amplitude 0.4, which completes eight cycles for every one of the slow wave. Because the two add, the peaks reach about 1.4. In the time domain the two ingredients are tangled together, and it is hard to read their exact frequencies or strengths from the curve. That is the motivation for the next step: the FFT separates them.
